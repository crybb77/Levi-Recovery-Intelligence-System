# Levi Recovery Intelligence System (LRIS)

## Vision

The Levi Recovery Intelligence System (LRIS) is a rehabilitation care management platform inspired by a real-world neurological recovery journey.

The goal of LRIS is to simplify the coordination of rehabilitation by providing caregivers, families, and healthcare professionals with a centralized, easy-to-use system for managing daily therapy, tracking progress, organizing documentation, and supporting informed decision-making.

Although inspired by Levi's rehabilitation, the platform is being designed with a modular and scalable architecture that can be adapted to support other individuals with neurological injuries or complex rehabilitation needs.

The project combines healthcare workflow design, data management, analytics, and future machine learning capabilities to improve communication, reduce caregiver onboarding time, and support long-term recovery.

---

## Core Design Principles

- User-friendly and intuitive
- Designed for caregivers and families
- Supports healthcare professionals with meaningful data
- Privacy and security conscious
- Modular and scalable
- Built using real-world rehabilitation workflows
- Machine learning ready
- Focused on improving quality of life

---

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to develop LRIS into a flexible rehabilitation platform capable of supporting personalized care planning, intelligent progress analysis, caregiver education, and AI-assisted decision support for neurological rehabilitation.
